Privacy policy - Harmonee

This policy describes how Silverbit Studio (“we”) handles information for Harmonee, a coming-soon connection app for matching, friendship, and dating.

What we may collect

When Harmonee becomes available, we may collect the information needed to create accounts, suggest matches, enable conversations, and keep the service safe:

  • check_circle
    Account and profile data Name, email, age eligibility, photos, bio, interests, intent, and preferences you choose to provide.
  • check_circle
    Connection and conversation data Suggestions, matches, messages, blocks, reports, and safety actions where those features are enabled.
  • check_circle
    Device and usage data Crash logs, diagnostics, security logs, and analytics used to improve reliability and prevent abuse.

Matching and AI support

Harmonee is designed around system suggestions instead of endless swiping. Matching may use profile signals, preferences, activity, safety signals, and AI-assisted ranking or summarization to help surface more relevant introductions. AI support is not a guarantee of compatibility, safety, or relationship outcome.

How we use information

We use information to operate Harmonee, create and rank suggestions, enable account and messaging features, investigate reports, improve product quality, and protect users from spam, impersonation, or harmful behavior. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing and service providers

We may share information with service providers that help us run infrastructure, authentication, analytics, messaging, moderation, support, and platform services. We may also disclose information if required by law or needed to protect users, the public, or Silverbit Studio.

Your rights and choices

Depending on your region, you may request access, correction, export, or deletion of your information. You can also edit your profile, remove optional details, block users, report safety issues, or request account deletion through the Silverbit Studio deletion page.
